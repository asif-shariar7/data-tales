### Data Science Essentials with Python

This repository contains the projects and practice exercises that I completed while learning step by step through **Cisco Networking Academy (NetAcad)'s** **[Data Science Essentials with Python](https://www.netacad.com/courses/data-science-essentials-with-python?courseLang=en-US)** course.

### Projects and Practice Exercise -

| Name | Notebook | What it explores |
|---|---|---|
| Menagerie Animal Word Trends | [`any-animal-except-project.ipynb`](./any-animal-except-project/any-animal-except-project.ipynb) | Investigating how British menageries (1750-1835) relate to animal-word usage by comparing exhibited animals with hard-to-keep ones. |
| A Plant-Based Coffee Shop | [`coffee-survey-project.ipynb`](./coffee-survey-project/coffee-survey-project.ipynb) | Analyzing a coffee survey to find the most popular dairy choices and recommend which plant-based milks a new specialty coffee shop should stock. |
| The Ocean's Deep-Diving Animals | [`deepest-divers-project.ipynb`](./deepest-divers-project/deepest-divers-project.ipynb) | Comparing the maximum diving depths of 118 marine animals across 10 categories, and turning a default Matplotlib bar chart into a clean, sorted horizontal chart. |
| Flight Delays | [`flight-delays-project.ipynb`](./flight-delays-project/flights-delays-project.ipynb) | Analyzing a sample of flights out of Atlanta airport to see how the day of the week affects the likelihood of a delayed departure (more than 15 minutes late). |
| Is Granola Healthy? | [`granola-healthy-project.ipynb`](./granola-healthy-project/granola-healthy-project.ipynb) | Comparing public vs. nutrition-expert opinion on 40 foods, using a scatter plot with an equality line to find where the two groups disagree the most. |
| The Internet and Dating | [`how-couples-met-project.ipynb`](./how-couples-met-project/how-couples-met-project.ipynb) | Investigating how the Internet changed the way couples meet from the 1960s to the 2010s, and building a focused multi-line graph that highlights the rise of online dating. |
| Lion Attacks | [`lion-attacks-project.ipynb`](./lion-attacks-project/lion-attacks-project.ipynb) | Investigating the relationship between evening moonlight and lion attacks on humans, and using lion belly-size data to explore a plausible causal mechanism (hunger) rather than stopping at correlation. |
| Plotting Animal Words | [`plotting-animal-words-project.ipynb`](./plotting-animal-words-project/plotting-animal-words-project.ipynb) | Using a Google Books word-frequency dataset (1700-2019) to plot animal-word trends and practice data storytelling, hypothesis testing, and the difference between correlation and causation. |
| Skeletal Variation | [`skeletal-variation.ipynb`](./skeletal-variation-project/skeletal-variation.ipynb) | Testing the claim that "over half our bones are in our hands and feet," then comparing neck-bone counts across mammals and birds to explore skeletal variation. |
| A Century of Top Songs | [`top-songs-project.ipynb`](./top-songs-project/top-songs-project.ipynb) | Tracking the length of the #1 song of the year from 1923 to 2023, parsing duration text into seconds, and finding the longest yearly chart-topper. |
| Tusked Elephants | [`tusked-elephants-project.ipynb`](./tusked-elephants-project/tusked-elephants-project.ipynb) | Comparing the tusk length of young male elephants before and after the poaching period, and controlling for age and body size using shoulder height. |
| Our World Connected | [`world-connected.ipynb`](./world-connected-project/world-connected.ipynb) | Exploring the rise of the Internet from 1990 to 2022 by combining internet-user counts with world population data to find when half the world became connected. |
